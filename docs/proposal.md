
# Phnom Penh Connect

## Smart Local Map and Navigation Application for Cambodia

**Project Category:** Smart City & Urban Services
**Hackathon Theme:** Theme 2 – Smart City & Urban Services
**Initial Target Area:** Phnom Penh, Cambodia
**Platform:** Mobile Application
**Project Stage:** Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

---

## 1. Project Overview

Phnom Penh Connect is a mobile-based local map and smart navigation application designed to help people travel around Phnom Penh more conveniently, safely, and efficiently.

The application focuses on solving local transportation and navigation problems that residents and visitors may experience in Phnom Penh. Unlike a general navigation application, Phnom Penh Connect aims to provide more locally relevant information, especially temporary road closures announced by the government, official road regulations, vehicle-specific road access, and local place discovery.

The application will help users find destinations, understand which roads they can legally use, receive notifications about temporary road closures, and discover alternative routes when their planned journeys are affected.

The initial development will focus on Phnom Penh, with the possibility of expanding to other cities and provinces in Cambodia in the future.

**Proposed tagline:** Navigate Smarter. Discover Locally. Travel Safely.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

People travelling around Phnom Penh may experience several transportation and navigation difficulties.

### 2.1 Temporary Road Closures

Government authorities sometimes announce temporary road closures for national events, public ceremonies, construction, security reasons, and other activities.

However, people may not receive this information before starting their journeys or reaching the affected roads.

This can result in:

- Unexpected detours.
- Wasted travel time.
- Increased traffic congestion around affected areas.
- Difficulty finding alternative routes.
- Inconvenience for people travelling to work, university, or other destinations.

### 2.2 Incorrect or Missing Road Sign Information

Some road signs may be missing, damaged, hidden, or difficult to notice.

For example, a driver may unknowingly enter a one-way road from the prohibited direction because the road sign is missing or unclear.

This can cause:

- Accidental traffic violations.
- Possible fines or penalties.
- Unsafe driving situations.
- Unnecessary travel delays.

Users need reliable road regulation information, even when physical road signs are difficult to see.

### 2.3 Different Road Access for Cars and Motorbikes

Not every road has the same access rules for different vehicle types.

For example:

- Some roads allow both cars and motorbikes.
- Some roads legally allow motorbikes but restrict cars.
- Some roads prohibit certain vehicle types.
- Some roads have one-way or no-entry restrictions.

Without suitable information, drivers may enter roads that their vehicle types are not legally permitted to use.

### 2.4 Difficulty Discovering Local Places

People may also have difficulty finding useful local destinations, especially when searching for specific services or places.

Examples include:

- Hospitals and clinics.
- Government offices.
- Universities and schools.
- Restaurants and cafés.
- Parking areas.
- Fuel stations.
- Markets and shopping centres.
- Public parks and tourist attractions.

A local map should make these places easier to discover and navigate to.

## 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of Phnom Penh Connect are:

1. Provide an accessible and useful local map for Phnom Penh.
2. Help users find destinations and navigate between locations.
3. Notify users about official temporary road closures that may affect their journeys.
4. Suggest alternative routes when roads are closed or restricted.
5. Provide road regulation information, including one-way roads, no-entry roads, and vehicle restrictions.
6. Support vehicle-based navigation for cars and motorbikes.
7. Help users discover important local places through categories and filters.
8. Improve travel convenience and help users avoid unnecessary detours and accidental traffic violations.

---

## 4. Target Users

The application is primarily designed for people who travel around Phnom Penh.

### Primary Users

- **Local residents:** People who regularly travel around Phnom Penh.
- **University students:** Students travelling between home, universities, and other destinations.
- **Workers and employees:** People commuting to workplaces.
- **Drivers and motorbike riders:** People who need road regulation information and suitable routes.

### Secondary Users

- **Visitors and tourists:** People who are unfamiliar with Phnom Penh.
- **Delivery riders:** People who need to discover destinations and avoid road restrictions.
- **New residents:** People who need help understanding the local road network and finding services.

# 5. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The initial MVP will focus on five main features.

## Feature 1: Accurate Local Map and Navigation

The application will provide an interactive map focused on Phnom Penh.

### Main Functions

- Display an interactive map of Phnom Penh.
- Show the user's current location using GPS.
- Search for streets, addresses, buildings, and landmarks.
- Allow users to select a destination.
- Provide directions from the user's current location to the destination.
- Display alternative routes when available.
- Show important roads and intersections.
- Allow users to return to their current GPS location.

### Main Purpose

Help people understand their surroundings, find destinations, and navigate around Phnom Penh more easily.

---

## Feature 2: Temporary Road Closure Alerts and Alternative Routes

**This is one of the most important and distinctive features of Phnom Penh Connect.**

The application will notify users when an officially announced temporary road closure may affect their planned journey.

Road closures may happen because of:

- National celebrations and public holidays.
- Government events and public ceremonies.
- VIP visits and security arrangements.
- Road construction and maintenance.
- Marathons, parades, and public gatherings.
- Flooding and emergency situations.
- Other temporary restrictions announced by relevant authorities.

### Main Functions

1. Receive road closure information from official government announcements or verified authorities.
2. Display affected roads on the map.
3. Show the reason for the closure when available.
4. Display the closure start and end times when officially provided.
5. Notify users whose planned routes or relevant travel areas are affected.
6. Avoid closed roads when calculating routes.
7. Suggest alternative routes.
8. Update route suggestions when new closure information becomes available.

### Example Scenario

A user plans to travel from home to university.

Before the user reaches a particular road, the government announces that the road will be temporarily closed from 7:00 AM to 12:00 PM because of a public event.

Phnom Penh Connect will:

1. Identify that the user's planned route is affected.
2. Send a road closure notification.
3. Highlight the affected road on the map.
4. Show the closure period and reason.
5. Suggest another available route that avoids the closure.

This allows users to make better travel decisions before reaching the affected road.

### Notification Types

- **Road Closed:** A road is officially closed.
- **Upcoming Closure:** A road will be closed in the future.
- **Traffic Warning:** An important traffic disruption or hazard has been reported.

### Important Considerations

- Official government announcements should be distinguished from unverified information.
- The application should show the information source and last update when available.
- Closure notifications should be relevant to users rather than being sent to everyone.
- The application should not display an estimated reopening time as confirmed unless an official source provides it.
- Government data access or integration is a future coordination requirement and is not assumed to exist at the beginning of the project.

### Main Purpose

Prevent unnecessary detours and help people plan their journeys more conveniently when temporary road closures occur.

---

## Feature 3: Smart Road Regulation Navigation

This feature helps users understand road regulations and avoid entering roads that are not legally accessible to them.

### Road Information

The application aims to include verified information about:

- One-way roads.
- No-entry roads.
- Prohibited turns.
- Prohibited U-turns.
- Vehicle-specific road restrictions.
- Other relevant road access restrictions.

Speed limits and no-parking information may also be included when reliable information is available.

### Main Functions

1. Display road direction and access restrictions.
2. Identify one-way roads.
3. Prevent routes from directing users against one-way traffic.
4. Warn users before they approach restricted roads or intersections.
5. Suggest alternative legal routes.
6. Keep road regulation information separate from physical road sign conditions.

### Example Scenario

A person is travelling to university and approaches a one-way road.

The physical one-way sign is missing or difficult to notice.

However, the application already has verified information that the road is officially one-way.

The application will:

1. Recognize the road restriction.
2. Warn the user before entering the prohibited direction.
3. Recommend a legal alternative route.
4. Help prevent an accidental traffic violation.

### Main Purpose

Help drivers understand official road regulations and avoid unnecessary penalties and unsafe driving situations.

---

## Feature 4: Vehicle-Based Navigation (Cars and Motorbikes)

The application will support different vehicle types when calculating routes.

Users can select their preferred vehicle:

- Car.
- Motorbike.

### Main Functions

- Identify roads that allow cars and motorbikes.
- Identify roads that allow motorbikes but legally restrict cars.
- Avoid roads that prohibit the selected vehicle type.
- Respect one-way and no-entry regulations.
- Suggest alternative routes based on vehicle access restrictions.

### Example Scenario

A user selects a car as their vehicle.

The application should avoid roads that legally prohibit cars, even if motorbikes are allowed.

If another user selects a motorbike, the application may use those roads when they are legally accessible.

### Important Consideration

A narrow road does not automatically mean cars are legally prohibited.

The application must distinguish between:

- **Legal restrictions:** Official rules that determine which vehicles may enter a road.
- **Physical road suitability:** Road width, surface conditions, and other physical characteristics.

The navigation system should prioritize verified legal restrictions rather than assuming that every narrow road prohibits cars.

### Main Purpose

Provide more suitable and legally compliant routes for different vehicle types.

# 7. Future Development

The following features are not part of the initial MVP. They may be considered after the main mapping and navigation services are established.

### 7.1 Community Road Reporting

Allow users to report:

- Missing or damaged road signs.
- Broken traffic lights.
- Potholes and damaged roads.
- Flooded roads.
- Blocked sidewalks.
- Broken streetlights.
- Road construction and other hazards.

Reports would need proper verification before being treated as reliable information.

**Reason for future development:**

The primary purpose of the MVP is local mapping and navigation. Road infrastructure reporting requires a verification process and potentially coordination with government authorities. Including it immediately could make the initial project too broad and difficult to manage.

### 7.2 Government Collaboration

Potential collaboration with relevant authorities, such as:

- Phnom Penh Capital Hall.
- Phnom Penh Department of Public Works and Transport.
- Traffic police.
- Other relevant municipal departments.

Possible future collaboration includes receiving verified information about road closures, road regulations, and transportation updates.

### 7.3 Advanced Live Traffic Information

- More detailed real-time traffic conditions.
- Better traffic congestion detection.
- Improved route recommendations based on traffic conditions.

### 7.4 AI-Powered Road Sign Recognition

Use image recognition to help identify road signs and detect possible missing or damaged signs.

### 7.5 Expansion Across Cambodia

After establishing the service in Phnom Penh, the application could expand to:

- Siem Reap.
- Battambang.
- Sihanoukville.
- Other Cambodian cities and provinces.

# 8. Mobile Application Interface Plan

The application will be designed primarily for mobile phones.

The interface should be simple, easy to understand, and suitable for everyday use.

### Main Navigation Sections

The application will have five main sections.

| Section | Purpose                                         |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Map     | Main map and navigation                         |
| Explore | Discover places and categories                  |
| Alerts  | View road closures and traffic notifications    |
| Saved   | Access saved locations                          |
| Profile | Manage account, language, vehicle, and settings |

### 8.1 Main Map Screen

The map screen is the application's primary interface.

It should include:

- Search bar.
- Interactive map.
- Current GPS location.
- Vehicle selector.
- Road closure indicators.
- Traffic information when available.
- Current location button.
- Bottom navigation bar.

When users search for or select a destination, a bottom information panel will display destination details and a Get Directions action.

### 8.2 Road Closure Alert Screen

This screen will display:

- Official closure announcements.
- Affected road names and locations.
- Reasons for closure.
- Closure start and end times when provided.
- Alternative route information.

### 8.3 Explore Screen

This screen allows users to discover local places through categories, search, and filters.

### 8.4 Saved Screen

Users can save frequently visited places, such as:

- Home.
- University.
- Workplace.
- Favourite restaurants.
- Other important destinations.

### 8.5 Profile and Settings Screen

Possible settings include:

- Khmer and English language options.
- Default vehicle selection.
- Notification preferences.
- Location permissions.
- Map preferences.
- Account settings.
- Privacy settings.

### Interface Behaviour

The recommended design combines two map layouts:

- **Normal map mode:** A full-screen map with floating controls.
- **Search and destination mode:** A larger bottom information panel that displays place or route details.

This allows users to see more of the map when navigating while still having access to useful information when needed.

# 11. Challenges and Considerations

The project may face several challenges.

1. **Accurate map data:** Road layouts, intersections, and place information must be sufficiently accurate.
2. **Official information access:** Accessing government road closure announcements and verified road regulation data may require coordination with authorities.
3. **Information updates:** Temporary road closures must be updated and removed when they expire or are officially cancelled.
4. **Data reliability:** The application must distinguish verified official information from unverified information.
5. **Routing accuracy:** The system must respect road direction and vehicle restrictions.
6. **Location permissions:** Users need to understand and grant location access for GPS navigation and relevant alerts.
7. **User experience:** The interface must remain simple and easy to use, especially while users are travelling.

---

# 12. Project Vision

The long-term vision of Phnom Penh Connect is to become a useful local mapping and navigation platform for Cambodia.

Rather than simply showing roads and destinations, the application aims to help people understand local transportation conditions before and during their journeys.

By combining local mapping, official road regulation information, temporary road closure notifications, vehicle-based routing, and place discovery, Phnom Penh Connect aims to make travelling around Cambodian cities more convenient, informed, and safer.
